WEBSITE POLICY

Privacy policy.

Draft policy for review before public launch.

Information collected

When you submit a registration or inquiry, the website may collect your name, email address, phone number, organisation, course preferences and the information you choose to provide.

Website chat

If you use the website chat, the messages you submit and the automated replies are stored in the website database so the conversation can continue and the administrator can review support requests. The chat does not use an external generative AI provider. Do not submit passwords, payment details or other sensitive information through chat.

Website analytics

Optional first-party traffic measurement runs only after you allow analytics in the privacy banner or settings. If you reject optional analytics, this measurement does not create its analytics browser identifier or send analytics events. You can reopen Privacy settings using the control at the bottom-left of the website. When enabled, the measurement stores a randomly generated browser identifier in local storage and records a hashed version with the page path, coarse device category and event time. It does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, chat message contents or full referrer URLs for analytics. First-party analytics records are retained for up to 90 days. Cloudflare Web Analytics is operated separately and may be subject to its own configuration and privacy practices.

Essential storage and preferences

The website uses essential browser storage for functions you request, including maintaining a support chat session. Your privacy choice is stored locally so the website can remember whether optional first-party analytics is allowed. Essential storage remains active when optional analytics is rejected.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to inquiries, review training registrations, communicate about courses and maintain administrative records.

Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the website, database and communications. The final provider list and applicable locations must be confirmed before publication.

Retention and security

Records should be retained only as long as needed for the stated purposes or applicable legal requirements. Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards will be used. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your requests

To ask about your information or request a correction, contact sohelrahman118@gmail.com.

This is a working draft, not legal advice. Confirm the data controller details, retention periods, applicable Bangladesh legal requirements and all third-party processors before publishing as a final policy.
